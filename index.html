Eight different chases. Eight different costumes. One destination. The job, the partner, the recognition, the legacy, they were never the target. They were bets, placed on the hope that they'd deliver a feeling.

Which means the chase itself was never the point. The feeling underneath it was.

So ask the better question:
"What emotional state am I optimizing for, and is this the most direct path to it?"
